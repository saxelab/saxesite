I am a PhD student at the [Computational and Biological Learning Lab](https://cbl.eng.cam.ac.uk/) at the University of Cambridge, co-supervised by [Yashar Ahmadian](https://cbl-website.onrender.com/ahmadian/people/ya311) and Andrew Saxe.

How do brains and machines retain and reuse knowledge across a lifetime of experience? I am interested in deriving simple theoretical principles that shed light on this question. My work so far has studied [the dynamics of rich and lazy learning in linear networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14623), and extended [this rich–lazy dichotomy to the continual learning setting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20062). I am currently exploring a theory of machine unlearning, and the relationship between an agent's affordances and the representations it builds of the world.
